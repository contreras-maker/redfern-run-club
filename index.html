Good to know

Do I need to be fast?

Not at all. There are no pace requirements. Walk it, jog it or race it. Nobody is left behind.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. Both runs are free, there are no membership fees and there is no sign-up.

When and where are the runs?

Tuesdays at 07:00 am from Innovation Plaza in South Eveleigh, and Fridays at 06:15 am from Gibbons Street Reserve in Redfern. Both runs are 5km.

Do I need to fill anything in first?

Just one short waiver, once, before your first run. It takes about a minute.

What if the weather is bad?

Weather calls are posted in the WhatsApp group, along with run times and event updates.

What happens after the run?

Coffee. The Friday run finishes at YohoLoco, and everyone is welcome to stay for a chat.

How do I join?

Join the WhatsApp group, say hello, then turn up on a Tuesday or Friday.
